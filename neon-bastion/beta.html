Test Neon Bastion

A roguelike tower defense. You are not the builder. You are the tower.

Neon Bastion is finished enough to play and not finished enough to release. It needs people on real phones before it goes out, because so far it has been tested on almost none.

It is a single-player Android tower defense: one position to hold, a blessing chosen after every wave, and permanent growth between runs. It plays offline, needs no account, and has no ads and nothing to buy today. Anything added later stays optional and will never gate progress behind money.

Three steps

  1. Join the tester group. Open the Google Group and join it with the same Google account you use on your phone. Google Play reads the membership list from this group, so joining with a different account will not work.
  2. Opt in on Google Play. Open the tester link and accept the invitation. You may need to wait a few minutes after joining the group.
  3. Install and play. The same link gives you a Play Store page to install from. Play a few runs over the next couple of weeks and tell us what you find.

What helps most

You do not have to write a report. One honest message is worth more than a checklist. The things we genuinely cannot find out without you:

  • Your phone model, and whether the game runs smoothly on it.
  • Heat and frame rate, especially once your main weapon is upgraded and the screen is busy. This is the specific thing we are trying to confirm.
  • Where you got stuck or bored. Which stage, and what you tried.
  • Anything confusing in the menus, the Memory map or the Forge.
  • Crashes or lost progress, with roughly what you were doing.

Send it to floridaairborne@gmail.com or post it in the group.

The honest small print

Google requires at least twelve testers to stay opted in continuously for fourteen days before an app can apply for public release. If you leave the group early the clock resets for your place, so if you would rather not commit, it is genuinely more useful to say no than to join and drop out.

This is a volunteer playtest. There is no payment and nothing to buy, and we will never ask you to click an advertisement. You can leave at any time by leaving the group and uninstalling. Your progress is stored on your own device; see your data for how to remove it.
