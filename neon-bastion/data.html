Your data and how to delete it

Last reviewed October 25, 2026, for Neon Bastion 0.103.1.

Neon Bastion has no account system and no server. Everything the game knows about you is a file on your own phone. There is nothing for us to hold, and nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never receive it.

What the game stores, on your device

Game progress, currencies, research, unlocked skills, enemy knowledge, stage records, equipment, skill-pool choices, settings and any suspended run, plus local recovery copies used to restore a save after an interrupted write. The game does not send any of it to us.

How to delete it yourself

  • Reset progress inside the game: Settings → Hard reset.
  • Remove the files: clear the app’s storage in Android settings.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app. The Android build has app-data backup disabled, so uninstalling normally removes its local data.
  • Browser version: clear the site’s stored data in your browser.

If you used Export progress, that code went wherever you pasted it — a note, a message, a file. Those copies are outside the game and have to be deleted from those places separately.

If you have emailed us

Support correspondence is the only thing we actually hold: your email address and whatever you chose to put in the message. To have it deleted, write to floridaairborne@gmail.com from the same address and say so. You do not need to give a reason. We keep support messages only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

Beta testers

If you joined the tester Google Group, your membership is held by Google under its own privacy policy. Leave the group to remove it. Google Play also records that you opted in to testing; that is part of your Play account, not something we store.

If this changes

The game currently has no sign-in, no cloud save, no advertising and no analytics. If any of those are added, this page and the privacy policy will be updated before the feature ships, and this page will carry the account deletion route as well as the device one.
